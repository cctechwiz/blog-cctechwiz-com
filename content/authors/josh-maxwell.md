## Who am I?

Hi, I am Josh Maxwell, also known online as CCTechWiz. My pronouns are he/him, I'm 6 feet tall with a bit of a dad bod and short brown hair that is usually spiked up when I'm not wearing a ball cap.

I am the CISO and a Principal Engineer at Quantizr, where I lead our infrastructure, security, and backend engineering work. My current languages of choice are Go, Typescript, and Bash. I also have quite a bit of experience with C++, Python, C, and x86 Assembly.

My personal mission is to multiply engineering impact through better tools, tighter security, and smarter AI integration. I care about building the infrastructure, security programs, and AI-powered workflows that let teams move fast without breaking things, because great engineering isn't just about writing code. It's about creating the environment where everyone can do their best work.

Before Quantizr, I spent four years at Qualtrics, most recently as a Senior Software Engineer and Team Lead on the Common Engineering Toolkit team, building and operating the tools all engineers there relied on.

Prior to Qualtrics, I worked as a Cyber Resiliency R&D Computer Engineer at Sandia National Laboratories. There I was focused on building applications and system integrations to support cyber resiliency R&D, experimentation, and execution.

I have a Master's degree in Information Security (MSIS) from Carnegie Mellon University, with an emphasis in secure software development and exploitation. I also have a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Southern Utah University. However, beyond my degrees, I have always had a deep craving for knowledge, to which I attribute the lion’s share of my most valuable experiences.

I'm also more than just my profession. I am married to a strong, amazing woman and we have two beautiful daughters. My other interests and hobbies include; personal knowledge management/productivity, running and playing various tabletop RPGs, managing a small homelab, dabbling in indie game development, reading, and mountain biking.
